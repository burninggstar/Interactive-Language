The Rise and Fall of a Midwest Princess (2026)

Laserprinted digital design

In Typographic Systems II, I redesigned this popular album only using typographic elements. I used opposing typefaces and colors to emphasize the duality of songs heard on the album and to also feel like the joyous, celebration of girlhood that the album is.
