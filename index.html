What Am I Doing?

Currently, I'm studying at the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville, Arkansas for a BFA in Graphic Design. Alongside this, I have minors in both Art History and Communication as well.

Outside of the classroom, I am a Graphic Designer for Student Affairs, a Volunteer Coordinator for Passionate About Literacy, and a Director of Ambassador Engagement for the School of Art. I keep myself busy, but beyond all of this, I also like to read, play video games, and hang out with friends.


What Do I Want To Do?

After graduation, my plans are to attend graduate school. I want to explore different areas of design and see new parts of the world. Within my career, I want to make intentional designs that carry weight.
