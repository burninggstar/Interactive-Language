Delanie (2025)

Acrylic paint on canvas

This was a custom piece created for one of my close friends. The intention behind it is to represent her through an indirect portrait. The image contains her favorite foods, colors, and flowers. These culminate together to manifest her inner character and emotions.


Self-Portrait (2024)

Acrylic paint on canvas

This piece represents the continual cycle that is being an artist. As I age my skills evolve, and how I perceive myself changes as well. My current being is based upon the culmination of everyone I've ever been before. My actions today are only possible because of the actions I made in the past. Through this painting, I wanted to showcase how I am still actively in the process of growing and learning, both artistically and personally.
